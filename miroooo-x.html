    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about the Miroooo X sonic toothbrush and our satisfaction guarantee.

    What sets Miroooo X apart?

    Miroooo X offers premium oral care with style. Powered by advanced sonic technology with 40,000 vibrations per minute, it removes up to 10x more plaque than manual brushing, with an elegant unibody aluminum design, 60-day battery life, and ultra-quiet operation.

    Is an electric toothbrush better than a manual toothbrush?

    Yes, clinical studies prove that sonic electric toothbrushes are significantly more effective at eliminating plaque and improving gum health. Miroooo X delivers 40,000 micro-vibrations per minute, cleaning deeper and far more consistently than manual brushing.

    Is Miroooo X suitable for sensitive teeth and gums?

    Absolutely! Miroooo X features multiple brushing modes, including a dedicated sensitive mode paired with ultra-soft DuPont rounded bristles to gently massage gums while protecting delicate tooth enamel.

    Can I subscribe for replacement brush heads?

    Yes, replacement brush head sets are readily available on our site or via recurring delivery so you always have fresh, hygienic bristles ready every 3 months.

    What is your warranty and return policy?

    We stand behind every Miroooo X with our two-year limited warranty and a 90-day home trial. If you're not completely thrilled, reach out to us for an effortless resolution.
