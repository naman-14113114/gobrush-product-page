    Traditional electric toothbrushes are often bulky, noisy, and constantly run out of battery right when you need them. We engineered the Miroooo X to redefine daily oral care through sleek minimalist design, aerospace metallurgy, and clinical-grade sonic performance.

    Powered by a high-efficiency magnetic levitation motor delivering 32,000 micro-vibrations per minute, the Miroooo X creates micro-bubble fluid dynamics that penetrate deep into interdental gaps, lifting away up to 10x more plaque and surface stains than manual brushing without harmful enamel abrasiveness.
