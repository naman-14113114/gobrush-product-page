Choosing a model

Before you order.

What is the difference between Miroooo X and Miroooo X2?
Miroooo X is the lighter, simpler model with three modes and 60+ days of battery life. X2 adds a pressure-sensor ring, 45° sweep guidance, IPX7 water resistance and up to 90 days of battery life. Our model comparison places the details side by side.
Which model should I choose?
Choose X if you want a straightforward, lightweight sonic brush. Choose X2 if pressure feedback and the longest battery interval matter more to you.
What comes in the box?
The exact contents are listed beside the buying controls on each product page. Both models include the toothbrush essentials, charging cable and travel protection; check your chosen bundle before paying.
Are replacement brush heads available?
Replacement-head availability will appear in the shop when stocked. If you need help before then, email support@miroooo.co.

Using your brush

Daily care.

How often should I charge it?
Miroooo X is designed for 60+ days of typical use between charges; X2 is designed for up to 90 days. Actual battery life varies with mode, pressure, session length and charging habits.
How do I clean the toothbrush?
After brushing, rinse the brush head and wipe the handle clean. Let both dry with airflow around them. Do not place a wet handle into a closed travel case for long periods.
Can I use it in the shower?
Miroooo X2 carries an IPX7 water-resistance specification. Water resistance does not mean the charging connection should be exposed to water; make sure the brush is dry before charging. Follow the care instructions supplied with your model.
What does the X2 pressure ring do?
It provides visible feedback intended to help you notice when you are applying excess pressure. It does not replace advice from your dentist or hygienist.

Orders & support

After checkout.

How long does UK delivery take?
Orders normally take 1–3 business days to process. Tracked transit is usually 3–10 business days after dispatch. These are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates.
How do I track my order?
Use the tracking link in your dispatch email. You can also visit the order-tracking page for guidance if your link has not arrived.
Can I return my toothbrush?
Orders include a 90-day home trial. Contact us within 90 days of delivery before sending anything back so we can confirm eligibility and provide the correct return instructions.
What does the warranty cover?
The two-year limited warranty covers manufacturing faults under normal household use. It does not cover ordinary wear, accidental damage, misuse, unauthorised repairs or consumable brush heads. See the full warranty page.
