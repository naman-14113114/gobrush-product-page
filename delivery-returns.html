UK delivery

Processing and transit.

Standard tracked delivery to UK addresses is free unless a different charge is shown at checkout. Orders normally take 1–3 business days to process. After dispatch, tracked transit is usually 3–10 business days.

Delivery dates are estimates rather than guarantees. Weekends, bank holidays, weather, customs checks, address issues and carrier disruption can affect timing.

When the order is dispatched, the carrier link is sent to the email address used at checkout. Please check your junk folder before contacting support.

Changes & delays

Tell us quickly.

If an address or order detail is wrong, email support@miroooo.co as soon as possible. We cannot guarantee a change once fulfilment has started.

If tracking has not updated for several business days or the estimated transit window has passed, send us the order number, checkout email and delivery postcode so we can investigate with the carrier.

90-day home trial

Start the return with us.

You may ask to return an eligible Miroooo toothbrush within 90 days of delivery. Contact support before sending anything back. We will confirm eligibility and provide the authorised return instructions and address.

Unless the item is faulty, incorrectly supplied or consumer law requires otherwise, return postage is the customer’s responsibility. Use a tracked service and keep the receipt until the refund is complete.

Return the handle, accessories, gifts and original components supplied with the order. Consumable replacement brush heads that were supplied in sealed packaging must remain sealed unless they were included with the toothbrush being trialled.

Do not return an item to an address found on a parcel label without contacting us. Fulfilment and return locations may differ, and an unauthorised parcel can be delayed or lost.

Refunds

Inspection and repayment.

Once the authorised return is received, it is inspected and we will email you with the outcome. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method. Banks and payment providers can take additional time to display the funds.

We may make a reasonable deduction where returned goods show damage beyond what is necessary to assess and trial the product, subject to your statutory rights.

This policy is additional to, and does not limit, rights you may have under UK consumer law.

Problems on arrival

Faulty, damaged or incorrect items.

Inspect the parcel soon after delivery. If the item is damaged, faulty or not what you ordered, contact us promptly with your order number, a description and clear photos where helpful. We will assess the issue and provide the appropriate remedy.
