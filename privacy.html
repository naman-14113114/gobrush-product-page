This policy applies to miroooo.co and Miroooo order and support services. “Miroooo”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the store operator identified on your order confirmation and payment record.

Information we collect

Only what supports the store.

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • identity and contact details, such as name, email, telephone number and delivery address;
  • order details, selected products, price, currency and fulfilment status;
  • payment status and limited transaction references from the secure payment provider—we do not need to store full card details;
  • messages, warranty evidence and support history;
  • device, browser, referral, cookie and site-interaction information;
  • fraud-prevention and security signals.

How we use it

Clear operational purposes.

We use personal information to take and fulfil orders, provide delivery and returns, answer support requests, honour warranties, secure the store, prevent fraud, keep required business records and improve the service. Where permitted, we may also send marketing you requested; you can unsubscribe at any time.

Our lawful bases may include performing a contract, complying with legal obligations, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the store, and consent where consent is required.

Who receives it

Providers that help fulfil the service.

Information may be shared with the ecommerce and secure-checkout platform, payment processors, fulfilment partners, carriers, hosting and security providers, customer-support tools, professional advisers and public authorities where legally required.

These providers receive information appropriate to their role. Some may process information outside the UK; where required, suitable transfer safeguards should be used.

Retention and security

Kept only as needed.

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described, including fulfilment, support, warranty, fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations. We use reasonable organisational and technical measures, but no online service can promise absolute security.

Your rights

Access and control.

Depending on UK data-protection law and the circumstances, you may ask to access, correct, erase, restrict or obtain a copy of your personal information, or object to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on it and complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Contact

Privacy requests.

Email support@miroooo.co with “Privacy request” in the subject line. The legal operator and any required contact address will be identified in your order documentation.

This policy may change when store providers or legal requirements change. Material updates will be posted here.
