Everything you need to know about the new Brush X2 electric toothbrush and our satisfaction guarantee.

While the original Brush X1 established our ultra-quiet acoustic standard, the Brush X2 adds active 45° sweeping oscillations (the Bass brushing method), increases battery endurance from 60 days to 90 days, integrates a seamless gapless touch button with intelligent halo glow, and includes dual home wall-mount & portable travel storage.
