    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about the new Miroooo X2 sonic toothbrush and our satisfaction guarantee.

    What sets the Miroooo X2 toothbrush apart?

    The Miroooo X2 delivers next-generation oral care with its revolutionary 45° wide-angle sweep vibration system and smart sensing chip. Compared to the older Miroooo X, the X2 features a unibody pressure-sensitive button, an extended 90-day battery life, IPX7 full waterproof immersion, and an adaptive soft balance algorithm for 10x deeper plaque removal.

    How does the Miroooo X2 compare to the previous Miroooo X?

    While the original Miroooo X established our ultra-quiet sonic standard, the Miroooo X2 adds active 45° sweeping oscillations (the Bass brushing method), increases battery endurance from 60 days to 90 days, integrates a seamless gapless touch button with intelligent halo glow, and includes dual home wall-mount & portable travel storage.

    Is the Miroooo X2 suitable for sensitive teeth and gums?

    Absolutely! The Miroooo X2 is engineered with 90%+ rounded-tip bristles (0.12mm ultra-soft filament) and features an intelligent pressure sensor that automatically regulates vibration intensity if you press too hard, ensuring full gumline protection.

    How long does the battery last and how is it charged?

    Powered by a high-energy lithium cobalt oxide battery cell, a single full charge lasts up to 90 days (180 brushing sessions). It charges rapidly using the included universal USB-C cable and magnetic charging dock.

    What is your warranty and money-back guarantee?

    Every Miroooo X2 is backed by our two-year limited warranty and a 90-day home trial. If you are not completely delighted with your brushing results, contact us for a hassle-free return or replacement.
