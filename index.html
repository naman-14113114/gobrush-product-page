    FAQs

    What sets the GoBrush toothbrush apart?

    The GoBrush toothbrush offers premium oral care with style. Powered by the latest sonic technology, it removes up to 10x more plaque, with an elegant design that fits any aesthetic. Quality and style in one.

    Is an electric toothbrush better than a manual toothbrush?

    Yes, research proves that electric toothbrushes are significantly more effective at removing plaque and improving gum health. The GoBrush produces 40,000 sonic vibrations per minute, cleaning faster and more thoroughly than manual brushing.

    Is the GoBrush suitable for sensitive teeth and gums?

    Absolutely! The GoBrush features multiple brushing modes, including a dedicated mode for sensitive teeth and gums. You get a thorough clean without any discomfort, even with a sensitive mouth.
