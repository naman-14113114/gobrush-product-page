# GoBrush & Miro Luxury Product Page

High-performance e-commerce product landing page with full English localization, video gallery looping, interactive variant switching for Rose Gold, Grey, and Silver, and luxury feature sections from Miroooo and Miroshine.

## 🚀 Features
- **Authentic Theme & Typography**: Built with full genuine Shopify Concept theme styles and Didot typography.
- **Top Looping Gallery Video**: Clean autoplaying muted video with no controls.
- **Live Variant Selection**: Dynamic switching across Rose Gold, Grey, and Silver with cross-fade image updates, active swatches, and sticky buy bar synchronization.
- **Miroooo Luxury Sections**:
  - "DISCOVER THE ULTIMATE IN ORAL HYGIENE" Split Video
  - "Brush with Style & Precision" (4 feature pointers + center hero toothbrush)
  - "3 BRUSH FUNCTIONS, 1 EFFECTIVE TECHNOLOGY" Mode showcases
  - "LUXURIOUS PROFESSIONALISM" Split Video
- **Miroshine Video Carousel**:
  - "Real customers, real reactions." 5-video UGC Reels carousel with Flickity, auto-advance, sound controls, and active slide scaling.
